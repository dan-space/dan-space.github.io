Building a Better World,
Beyond Earth.

I started by imagining another world.
Then I tried to understand what it would take
to build one responsibly.

Where it began. When I was very young,
I watched my mother play StarCraft.
I was fascinated by a simple possibility:
people could leave Earth, gather resources,
build bases, and create a new civilization.
The question stayed with me —
but it became harder as I learned more.
✦   A childhood game became a long-term design question.

I was just a small child,
watching my mother play a game
called StarCraft.

She was building.
I was watching.
But the question stayed.
